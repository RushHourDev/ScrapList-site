Privacy Policy for Scrap List

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Scrap List (“the app”) is made by RushHourDev (“we”). This policy says what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect anything. Your lists stay on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps your list on your phone, in the app’s private storage:

  • the text of your items and whether they are struck through or pinned;
  • pictures you add to an item (taken with the camera, chosen from the gallery, or shared to the app from another app);
  • voice recordings you make, or recording files you choose;
  • your three settings (where new items go, the theme, and vibration on or off).

This information never leaves your phone because of the app. The app has no internet permission, so it can not send anything to us or to anyone else. We have no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Permissions

  • Microphone: asked for only when you tap “Record something”. The recording is saved on your phone, attached to the item you chose. Nothing is listened to in the background.
  • Camera and gallery: the app opens your phone’s own camera or picture chooser. It receives only the one picture you take or choose, and it does not ask for broad access to your photos.
  • Shared items: when you use Share and choose Scrap List, the app receives only the text, link or picture you shared, and adds it to your list.

Backups made by Android

Android can back up an app’s data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on in your phone’s settings. If so, your list may be included in that backup, handled by Google under your Google account and Google’s privacy policy. We do not receive it and can not see it. You can turn this off in your phone’s settings (Google > Backup).

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Delete an item inside the app, or clear the app’s storage or uninstall it in your phone’s settings, and the list, pictures and recordings are removed from the phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes

If the app ever changes what it does with information, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you.
