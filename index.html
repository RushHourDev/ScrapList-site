A scrap of paper for your phone

Quick throwaway lists on your phone! Open it, type your list, strike things off, and swipe to delete them! You can even include a quick photo or voice recording for each item in your list! Useful if you need to reference a quick picture or voice memo. Use this if you need to make a quick shopping list, or you’re running errands throughout the day!

Coming soon to Google Play

No account. No network. No Ads! Your list stays on your phone.

Tons of features!

  • Quickly add items in your list as soon as you launch the app!
  • Tap an item to strike through when you’re done with the task!
  • Swipe right to pin important tasks, or swipe left to delete them!
  • Hold the item to select several to delete in bulk!
  • Drag the handle on a row to reorder your items!
  • Undo if you accidentally delete something before you completed the task!
  • Add a picture or voice recording to help create your list!

Paste a whole list!

You can even paste an entire list in one go!

Copy this from a message or a note:

- Milk
- Eggs
1. Bread from the bakery

Batteries

Paste it into the add field, and you get four items:

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Bread from the bakery
  • Batteries

Share to Scrap List

Share pictures, text, or a link from other apps! Just tap Share and choose Scrap List to add it directly to your list!

Pictures and recordings

Attach a photo or a short voice recording (up to 1 minute) to any item!

Home-screen widget

Put your list on the home screen! Tap an item there to strike it through!

Customize your lists to the way you want!

  • Add items to the top or bottom!
  • Light or dark mode!
  • Add or remove a slight haptic feedback when using the app!

Made to be easy for everyone

Large touch targets, labels for screen readers (TalkBack), and text that scales up to three times the normal size.

Private by design

Scrap List has no internet permission at all, so it can not send your list anywhere by itself. Only when you tap the share icon does it hand your list to the app you choose. It asks for the microphone only when you tap “Record something”. Read the full privacy policy.
